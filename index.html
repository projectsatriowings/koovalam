Koovalam — Spiritual Wellness & Nature Retreat in Kerala

Koovalam is a calm wellness retreat in Kerala. We help you slow down and reconnect with nature. Our four-day retreat takes place in the green hills of Wayanad. Enjoy gentle yoga, quiet breathing, and fresh forest air. All sessions are simple and gentle. Anyone can join, from beginners to regular yoga lovers.

Three Dimensions of Well-Being: Body, Mind & Soul

Good health has three simple parts. These parts are the body, the mind, and the soul. Like the three leaves of the sacred Bilva tree, they work together. When you care for all three, you feel whole and peaceful.

Sharira (Body) — Natural Vitality

We move with gentle yoga postures. We eat fresh, warm vegetarian meals. We breathe clean mountain air to renew our energy.

Manas (Mind) — Mental Stillness

We calm our thoughts with quiet breathing. We sleep deeply with guided rest. We take time away from screens and phone calls.

Atman (Soul) — Inner Connection

We visit quiet old temples. We sit in silence by forest streams. We listen to soft music by the evening fire.

The Koovalam Retreat Experience in Wayanad

Our retreat gives you time to rest. There is no rush here. You wake up to the sound of birds. You see the mist roll over the green hills.

  • Gentle Yoga and Breath: We do easy morning stretches. We practice slow breathing to relax the body.
  • Nature Walks: We take peaceful walks through green tea gardens and forest paths.
  • Temple Peace: We visit the quiet Tirunelli Temple in the morning hills.
  • Evening Music: We sit by the campfire and listen to sweet flute tunes and acoustic songs.

Four-Day Retreat Itinerary & Daily Schedule

Each day gives you a good balance of group time and quiet rest. You can join every session or take quiet time for yourself.

  1. Day 1 — Arrival & Welcome: Check in after lunch. Settle into your cottage. Drink fresh herbal tea. Meet our team in the evening. Enjoy a warm dinner.
  2. Day 2 — Morning Light & Rest: Wake up early to see the sunrise above the clouds. Do gentle yoga on the wooden deck. Walk along forest paths. Rest deeply in the afternoon.
  3. Day 3 — Temple & River Visit: Visit an ancient temple in the morning hills. Sit by clean mountain streams. Learn about local tribes and plants in the afternoon. Listen to flute music by the campfire at night.
  4. Day 4 — Gratitude & Goodbyes: Start with a gentle stretch. Share your thoughts in a morning circle. Eat a tasty farewell lunch. Leave with a calm mind and happy heart.

Sanctuary Stay, Vegetarian Cuisine & Travel

You stay at Oxyfarm Resorts in Wayanad. The cottages are clean, airy, and quiet. Each room has large windows with hill views. You will love the pure mountain breeze.

Our food is 100% vegetarian. We cook with fresh local farm produce. We use clean water, mild spices, and coconut. The meals are light, tasty, and easy to digest.

Reaching the resort is easy. The closest airport is Kannur. The closest rail station is Thalassery. We can help you book a local taxi to the resort.

Retreat Pricing, Inclusions & Booking Details

Our pricing is clear and all-inclusive. There are no surprise costs. Everything you need for a restful stay is included:
