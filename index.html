Three Dimensions of Well-Being: Body, Mind & Soul

At Koovalam, human vitality unfolds through three harmonious dimensions: Sharira (Body), Manas (Mind), and Atman (Soul). Just as the sacred Bilva leaf comprises three indivisible leaflets, our retreat grounds you through integrated physical wellness, mental quietude, and spiritual awakening.

Sharira (Body) — Natural Vitality

Rebalance physical energy through gentle Hatha yoga postures, conscious breathwork, and wholesome vegetarian food freshly prepared with locally harvested Kerala herbs.

Manas (Mind) — Mental Stillness

Quiet mental turbulence with Pranayama breathing techniques, guided Yoga Nidra, silent walking meditation, and digital decompression amidst lush mountain mist.

Atman (Soul) — Inner Connection

Rediscover inner tranquility through sacred temple visits, Vedic chanting, ancestral heritage reflections, and quiet communion beside natural waters.

The Koovalam Retreat Experience in Wayanad

Set in the Western Ghats of Wayanad, Kerala, Koovalam offers an unhurried sanctuary far removed from modern urban haste. The 4-day, 3-night experience is designed for seekers, professionals, and nature lovers seeking authentic rejuvenation.

  • Gentle Yoga & Pranayama: Daily sunrise and sunset sessions guided by experienced practitioners, suitable for all skill levels.
  • Mindful Nature Walks: Meditative paths through private tea plantations, stream beds, and high-altitude forest canopies.
  • Temple Sanctuary Stillness: Contemplative visits to ancient Kerala shrines including the historic Tirunelli Temple and sacred groves.
  • Cultural Evenings: Intimate traditional Kerala arts, classical flute melodies, and philosophical fireside discussions.

Four-Day Retreat Itinerary & Daily Schedule

Each day is curated to balance structured mindfulness practices with generous personal downtime for reflection, reading, and rest.

  1. Day 1 — The Arrival & Grounding: Check-in at Oxyfarm Resorts, welcome herbal tea, orientation circle, gentle evening breathwork, and nourishing satvik dinner.
  2. Day 2 — Deep Awakening & Inner Clearing: Sunrise yoga on the mountain pavilion, pranayama techniques, silent nature trail, Ayurvedic dietary discussion, and Yoga Nidra relaxation.
  3. Day 3 — River & Temple Heritage: Morning mindful walk, excursion to sacred temple waters, quiet contemplative time, and traditional evening cultural recital.
  4. Day 4 — Renewal & Return: Morning gratitude practice, closing integration circle, celebratory brunch, and departure with personalized daily mindfulness routines.

Sanctuary Stay, Vegetarian Cuisine & Travel

Our retreat sanctuary is Oxyfarm Resorts in Mylattumala, Wayanad. Accommodations feature premium eco-friendly cottages and rooms with panoramic views of verdant hills and misty valleys.

Dining is centered on pure vegetarian, Ayurvedic-inspired Kerala meals prepared daily with seasonal vegetables, unpolished rice, coconut, and restorative spices that support digestion and vitality.

Convenient transport coordination is provided from Kuttipuram and Calicut railway stations and Kozhikode International Airport (CCJ).
