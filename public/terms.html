Clear Guidelines and Booking Agreement for Your Stay

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Welcome to Koovalam. These Terms explain how booking works, what is included in your stay, and how we keep our retreat calm and safe for everyone.

When you book a retreat with us or use our website at https://koovalam.com/, you agree to these simple terms.

1. About Koovalam

Koovalam is a holistic wellness brand rooted in Kerala. We offer peaceful nature retreats inspired by the sacred Bilva tree. Our goal is to help you rest, breathe fresh air, and feel calm.

All yoga and meditation classes are gentle and slow. You join only what you want. Nothing is forced.

2. Who Can Join

We want everyone to have a safe and restful stay:

  • Age Range: Guests should be between 18 and 60 years old. You should be able to care for yourself comfortably during your stay.
  • Small Groups: We take only a small number of guests per retreat. This keeps the atmosphere quiet and unhurried.
  • Solo Guests and Friends: You can come alone, with a friend, or with a partner. We welcome all people with warmth and care.

3. How Booking Works

Booking your spot is quick and straightforward:

A. Sending an Enquiry

Sending a form on our website is free. It does not force you to pay. It lets our team know you want to come. We will check available rooms and message you on WhatsApp or email.

B. Retreat Rates

Our published rates are all-inclusive:

  • Twin-Sharing Room: ₹17,999 per person. You share a room with two separate beds.
  • Private Single Room: ₹26,999 per person. You have your own private room and bathroom.

C. Confirming Your Room

Your room is confirmed once we receive your booking payment. You can pay via UPI or simple bank transfer. We will send you an official booking receipt right away.

4. What Is Included in Your Price

A. What We Provide

  • 3 nights cottage stay at Oxyfarm Resorts in Wayanad.
  • All pure vegetarian meals, snacks, and tea from Day 1 dinner to Day 4 lunch.
  • Clean herbal drinking water all day.
  • All daily yoga, breathwork, and deep rest sessions.
  • Mylattumala sunrise view and morning hilltop meditation.
  • Guided walk in the green tea and coffee gardens.
  • Trips to Tirunelli Temple, Meenmutty Waterfall, and the plant sanctuary.
  • Evening campfire music and reflection circles.

B. What Is Not Included

  • Your personal flight, train, or bus tickets to Wayanad.
  • Personal travel insurance or medical insurance.
  • Personal shopping, phone calls, or private laundry.
  • Optional private dance shows (Kathakali or Bharatanatyam), which can be booked on request.

5. Cancellation and Refund Rules

We book rooms and buy fresh food well before your arrival. If your plans change, please tell us as soon as possible:

  • 30 Days or More Before Arrival: Full refund minus a small 5% bank charge.
  • 15 to 29 Days Before Arrival: 50% cash refund, or 100% credit for any future retreat within one year.
  • Less Than 15 Days Before Arrival: No cash refund. However, you can pass your spot to a friend or family member with our approval.
  • Bad Weather or Travel Bans: If a major storm or government rule stops the retreat, we will move your booking to a new date for free.

6. Health & Medical Notice

Important Health Notice:

Our yoga, breathing, and meditation classes help you relax and feel good. They are not medical treatments. They do not replace doctor advice or hospital care.

Please tell us before you arrive if you have heart issues, back injuries, recent surgeries, or if you are pregnant. You can pause or skip any movement class at any time.

7. House Rules for a Peaceful Sanctuary

To keep the resort quiet and pleasant for all guests, we ask you to follow these simple rules:

  • No Alcohol or Drugs: Alcohol and illegal drugs are strictly banned on retreat grounds. Smoking is allowed only in small remote open areas away from rooms.
  • Quiet Nights: Please keep noise low from 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM so everyone can sleep deeply.
  • Respect for Local Culture: Please wear modest clothes covering shoulders and knees when we visit local temples.
  • Care for Nature: Please do not litter. Help us keep the green hills and clean mountain streams beautiful.

8. Travel Safety & Belongings

Our team works hard to make your trip safe and comfortable. However, Koovalam and Oxyfarm Resorts are not liable for:

  • Flight or train delays that make you arrive late.
  • Lost or broken personal items like phones, jewelry, or cash.
  • Injuries if you do not follow our safety tips during nature walks.

9. Legal Details

These Terms follow the laws of India. Any legal dispute will be settled in the courts of Kerala, India.
