Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

When you enquire or register for our spiritual wellness retreat, we collect personal contact details including your full name, email address, phone number, and retreat preferences (occupancy type, group size, and dietary considerations).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to facilitate your retreat registration, communicate booking details, answer enquiries, and coordinate your stay at Oxyfarm Resorts, Wayanad.

3. Data Protection and Confidentiality

Your data is stored securely and is never sold, leased, or distributed to unauthorized third parties. Access is restricted to designated retreat coordinators.

4. Third-Party Services

Our website utilizes secure communication providers for processing form submissions and customer service communication (e.g., WhatsApp Business). These services follow strict industry privacy standards.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or requests regarding your personal data, please contact our privacy officer at info.koovalam@gmail.com.
