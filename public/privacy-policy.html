Privacy Policy

How We Protect and Respect Your Personal Information

Last updated: November 26, 2026

At Koovalam, we care about your privacy. We want you to feel safe when you visit our website or book a retreat with us. This page explains in plain words what information we collect, why we need it, and how we keep it safe.

1. What This Policy Covers

This policy applies to:

  • Our website at https://koovalam.com/ and all its pages.
  • Our contact and booking forms.
  • Direct chats and emails with our team on WhatsApp or phone.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only what we need to plan your retreat. We never ask for unnecessary personal details.

A. Contact Details

  • Your full name and title.
  • Your email address and phone number.
  • Your city and country. This helps us plan arrival tips and travel support.

B. Retreat Details

  • Your room choice: Twin-Sharing Room or Private Single Room.
  • Number of guests joining you.
  • Food allergies and health needs. This helps our kitchen cook safe, tasty meals for you.
  • Your arrival time and flight or train details. This helps us welcome you on time.

C. Basic Web Data

When you view our site, our server logs basic technical data. This includes your browser type, device size, and pages visited. We use this data only to keep the website fast, secure, and working well on phones.

3. Why We Use Your Information

We use your information for simple and honest reasons:

  1. To Book Your Stay: We need your name to reserve your cottage at Oxyfarm Resorts in Wayanad.
  2. To Keep You Safe: We share your food needs with our cooks so all your meals are safe to eat.
  3. To Answer Your Questions: When you message us on WhatsApp or email, we use your details to reply.
  4. To Send Updates: We send you a simple packing list, driving directions, and arrival tips before you travel.

4. We Never Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not give your phone number or email to advertisers or marketing companies.

We share your name and room choice only with our resort hosts at Oxyfarm Resorts. They use it only to check you in and prepare your meals.

5. How We Protect Your Information

We protect your data using strong modern security tools:

  • HTTPS Encryption: All data sent to our website travels over a secure, encrypted link.
  • Safe Storage: Guest lists and forms are stored on secure servers with strong passwords.
  • Limited Access: Only our small retreat team can see your booking forms.

6. How Long We Keep Your Data

We keep your details only as long as needed. If you make an inquiry but do not book, we clear your details within one year. If you join our retreat, we keep basic payment records for tax laws, then delete them safely.

7. Your Rights Over Your Data

You have full control over your personal information:

  • See Your Data: You can ask us what details we have on file for you.
  • Fix Your Data: You can ask us to update your phone number, email, or arrival time.
  • Delete Your Data: You can ask us to delete your contact details at any time.
  • Stop Messages: You can tell us to stop sending emails or WhatsApp updates whenever you wish.

8. Children's Privacy

Our retreat is designed for adults aged 18 to 60. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18 years of age.
