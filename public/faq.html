Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About the Koovalam Retreat in Wayanad

Here are simple answers to common questions about our retreat. We explain our location, rooms, food, daily yoga, and booking steps. We want you to feel fully prepared and at ease.

01. Retreat Location & Dates

Where we stay and how to reach us

Where is the retreat held?

The retreat takes place at Oxyfarm Resorts in Wayanad, Kerala. The resort is set on the green hills of Mylattumala. It borders the calm Periya forest.

There is no city noise here. You hear birds singing at dawn and wind in the trees. The air is cool and fresh all year.

How many days is the retreat and what are the dates?

The retreat lasts for 4 days and 3 nights. It runs from December 25 to December 28.

Check-in starts at 2:30 PM on Day 1. You will have time to settle into your room and drink fresh herbal tea. We finish at 2:00 PM on Day 4 after a warm farewell lunch.

How do I reach the resort?

You can travel by plane, train, or bus:

  • By Plane: Fly to Kannur International Airport (CNN). It is about 80 km away. A scenic drive takes about 2.5 hours.
  • By Train: The nearest major station is Thalassery Railway Station. It is about 80 km away.
  • By Bus: Mananthavady Bus Stand is just 14 km away from the resort.

Guests book their own travel tickets. If you need a taxi from the airport or rail station, our team can help you book one.

02. Rooms & Pure Vegetarian Food

Cottages, wholesome meals, and dietary care

What are the rooms like?

You stay in clean, comfortable nature cottages at Oxyfarm Resorts. Each room has private bathrooms with warm running water. You get large windows with open views of green hills and tea plants.

We offer two room options:

  • Twin-Sharing Room: ₹17,999 per person. You share a room with two separate beds. You can come with a friend or share with a fellow guest.
  • Private Single Room: ₹26,999 per person. You get your own private cottage and bathroom. This option is great for quiet rest.
What food do you serve?

We serve 100% vegetarian meals. Our cooks use fresh vegetables from local Kerala farms. The food is based on ancient Ayurvedic wisdom.

Meals are light, warm, and easy to digest. We use mild spices, coconut, and whole grains. Warm herbal drinking water is ready for you all day.

Can you handle food allergies?

Yes. Please tell us about your food needs when you book. We can handle lactose-free, nut-free, or mild non-spicy meals. Our kitchen team will take good care of you.

03. Yoga & Daily Practices

Gentle movement, breathwork, and voluntary sessions

Do I need to be good at yoga to join?

No. You do not need any past yoga experience. You do not need to be flexible.

Our yoga is slow and gentle. We focus on easy body stretches and calm breathing. Our teachers guide you step by step. You move only in ways that feel safe and good.

Are all classes compulsory?

No. Every class and walk is 100% voluntary. You can join what you like. If you want to sleep late, read a book, or sit on your porch, you are free to do so. This is your time to heal.

What sessions do we practice each day?

Our daily schedule includes:

  • Gentle Classical Yoga: Morning movement to wake up the body.
  • Breathing Practice: Simple breath exercises to quiet the mind and lower stress.
  • Sunrise Meditation: Sitting in peace above the morning clouds on Mylattumala hill.
  • Guided Deep Rest: Lying down for guided body relaxation before sleep.
  • Quiet Journaling: Time to write down your thoughts and set peaceful goals.

04. Excursions & Nature Walks

Old temples, waterfalls, and tea gardens

What trips are included in the retreat?

We take three gentle trips into nature:

  • Tirunelli Temple Trip: We visit this quiet, ancient forest temple in the morning hills. Guests who wish may sit by the sacred stream.
  • Waterfall and Plant Garden: We visit Meenmutty Waterfall and the Gurukula plant garden. You will see rare Western Ghats plants and clear streams.
  • Plantation Walk: We take a slow walk through green tea, coffee, and pepper plants around the resort.
What should I wear to the temple?

Please wear simple, modest clothes that cover your shoulders and knees. Cotton kurtas, loose pants, or long skirts work very well. We will share full tips before the visit.

Clear rates, inclusions, and how to book

06. Packing & Practical Tips

What to bring, weather, and phone signal

What should I pack for the retreat?

We suggest packing:

  • Loose cotton clothes for yoga and sitting.
  • Walking shoes or sports sneakers for trails.
  • A light sweater or warm shawl for cool hill mornings.
  • A refillable water bottle, a small journal, and personal toiletries.
Is there mobile network and Wi-Fi?

Yes. Airtel and Jio get good mobile signal at the resort. Wi-Fi is ready in the main lounge. We encourage guests to put away phones and enjoy the calm silence of nature.
