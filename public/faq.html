Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Koovalam 4-day spiritual wellness retreat at Oxyfarm Resorts, Wayanad.

Retreat Experience & Eligibility

What is the focus of the Koovalam retreat?

Koovalam unites natural living, gentle yoga, pranayama breathwork, meditation, and mindful temple experiences rooted in Kerala's living heritage. All sessions are non-dogmatic and voluntary.

Can beginners participate?

Yes. Our practices are structured for all experience levels, from complete beginners to experienced practitioners.

Accommodation & Dining

Where is the retreat held?

The retreat is hosted at Oxyfarm Resorts, set in the serene mist-clad hills of Mylattumala, Wayanad, Kerala.

What meals are provided?

Nourishing vegetarian meals prepared with locally sourced Kerala ingredients, herbal drinking water, and teas are included throughout the stay.

Pricing & Booking

What is the investment?

Double Occupancy is ₹17,999 per person; Single Private Occupancy is ₹26,999. Inclusions encompass 4 days/3 nights accommodation, all meals, daily yoga and meditation, and guided excursions.
